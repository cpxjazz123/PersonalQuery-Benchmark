```markdown
**Reproducibility.** Every quantitative claim in this paper has been audited against
the open-source release pipeline with both file-existence and value-validation checks
(see Abstract and §1 for the audit summary, Tables 1/2/3 inline footnotes for
per-table discrepancies, and §5 Limitations above for re-execution cost estimates).
The current audit covers 17 claims: 6 verified (file exists + extracted value matches
paper number within tolerance), 4 discrepant (Table 2 metrics with relative deltas
13.4–78.4% between paper and release values), 1 degenerate (Table 1 bootstrap CI
all-NaN due to Stage 6 query-pool size; see Table 1 footnote), 5 unverified (Table 3
+ §2.2 infrastructure claims due to Stage 12 outputs not being persisted in the
open-source release; see Table 3 footnote), and 1 partial (env-var feature with no
output artifact). Re-running Stage 12 (≈45–135 min per category on GPU, ≈9–21 h total
for all three domains) would flip the 5 unverified claims to verified; re-running
Stage 6 + Stage 9 with the full ≈90-query pool (≈30–60 min per category, ≈1.5–3 h
total) would flip the 1 degenerate claim to verified and yield non-degenerate
bootstrap confidence intervals. The full per-claim audit, including extracted values,
expected values, absolute deltas, and relative deltas, is available at
`result/personal_query/iterations/paper_claims_audit.json` and is regenerated by
`python3 PersoanlQuery/paper_claims_audit.py` against the open-source release
artifacts. We encourage readers to re-run this audit after any code or release change
to verify reproducibility.
```
